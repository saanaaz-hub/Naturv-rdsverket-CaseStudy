## 🎯 Executive Summary
Traditional environmental agency communication often relies on dense PDFs and passive reports that fail to engage private individuals and housing cooperatives (BRFs). **Att nå flera** ("Reaching More Citizens") is an agile product initiative designed for Naturvårdsverket. The goal: *Turn biodiversity guidance into simple, local, social actions that citizens actually want to take.*

This case study covers end-to-end product development—from persona creation and user journey mapping to backlog prioritization, capacity management, and a fully functional MVP built in Lovable.

---

## 🔍 1. The Problem & Discovery
* **The Gap:** Engagement drops to zero when campaigns end. Citizens want to help biodiversity ("what can I do this weekend?"), but face friction, complex expert language, and a lack of localized, actionable guidance.
* **The Challenge:** *How might we turn biodiversity guidance into simple, local, social actions that private individuals and BRFs actually want to take?*

---

## 👥 2. Target Audience & Personas
Instead of relying on broad demographics, the product segments users by **scale of decision** (individual vs. group) and **expertise**:
1. **Anders (Villa Owner):** Primary target for Sprint 1. Has space and intent, but lacks a local, step-by-step plan for his lawn.
2. **Karin (BRF Board Member):** Acts on behalf of a housing cooperative. Needs tools to pitch to the board and rally neighbors.
3. **Lina (First-time Balcony Gardener):** Small space, zero experience. Needs pot-friendly flora and on-demand guidance.

---

## 🏗️ 3. Product Architecture: Two Features, One App
To solve for different scales of decision-making, the experience is split into two core flows:
* **Min Backyard Äng (Individual):** Postal-code seed recommendations, step-by-step lawn-to-meadow conversion, balcony pot mode, and an AI gardening coach.
* **Grön Granne (Community/BRF):** Courtyard registration, gamified level progression (*Frö → Grodd → Blomma → Äng*), checklists, and leaderboards.

---

## 🏃‍♂️ 4. Agile Delivery & Sprint Strategy
Managed with a team velocity of **35 points per 2-week sprint**, utilizing INVEST user stories (*Given/When/Then*):

* **Sprint 1 (22 points):** Postal-code seed mixes, balcony mode, BRF courtyard registration, initial Lovable MVP build, and usability testing with 10 citizens.
* **Sprint 2 (36 points - *Capacity Adjusted*):** AI gardening coach, BRF checklists, leaderboards, and seasonal reminders. *(Proactive PM trade-off: Ran planning poker and shifted the impact dashboard to Sprint 3 to resolve over-capacity).*
* **Sprint 3 (14 points):** Karin’s journey—board meeting toolkits, planting day invites, and a nearby courtyards map.
* **Release 2 (January 2027):** Naturvårdsverket official certification, physical signage, and BRF peer mentoring handovers.

---

## 💻 5. The MVP (Built in Lovable)
Using a comprehensive prompt structure defining personas, data models, and UI flows, I generated a working MVP of the application. 
* **Key User Flow Tested:** Entering a postal code to instantly receive tailored seed recommendations and step-by-step guidance.
* **Usability Testing Plan:** Designed to test 5 core hypotheses across 10 participants representing Anders, Karin, and Lina.

---

## 📊 6. Success Metrics (KPIs)
* 🚀 **10,000 m²** of lawn converted in Year 1.
* 🔄 **40%** 30-day user return rate.
* 🤖 **≥ 80%** helpful rating on AI gardening coach responses.
* 🌱 **10 pilot BRFs** confirmed, with $\ge 3$ reaching *Grodd* level within 30 days.
